Pop Quiz for Google Slides

The team quiz that lives in your slides

People answer on their phones. The votes, the clock and the leaderboard appear on your own slides, in your deck's design.

This is the early version, for testers. You need a Mac, Chrome and a Google Slides deck. Phones need nothing: they scan a code.

  1. Fetch it

    Open Terminal and paste this. It puts Pop Quiz in a folder called PopQuiz in your home folder, and nowhere else.

  2. Load it in Chrome, once

    Open chrome://extensions in a new tab. Turn on Developer mode, top right. Press Load unpacked and choose the app folder inside PopQuiz.

    Chrome won't let a web page do this step for you.

  3. Open your deck

    Reload any Google Slides tab that was already open. Click the Pop Quiz icon in Chrome's toolbar. It sits under the puzzle piece until you pin it. The panel opens beside your deck.

  4. Build your quiz

    Pick a slide and choose what it does in the panel: a question, a poll, a join screen or a leaderboard. Then press Google's own Slideshow button. People join by scanning the code on your join screen.

Keeping it current

When a newer Pop Quiz is out, the panel tells you. Paste the line from step 1 again, then press Reload Pop Quiz in the panel. Your quizzes are kept.

Or have your Mac look for a newer one every quarter of an hour by itself:

To stop that: sh ~/PopQuiz/popquiz.sh --stop

Before you rely on it

  • It's free, and in testing. Run it once with a few phones before you use it in front of a room.
  • Your quiz stays on your laptop. It's kept by Chrome, and a safety copy goes into your Downloads folder after every change.
  • Players give a nickname and nothing else. No accounts, no app.
  • Up to 100 phones a game. It works in the room and over a video call.
  • If it stops mid-game, a show of hands still works.
